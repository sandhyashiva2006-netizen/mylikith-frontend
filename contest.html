Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?

Any verified MyLikith writer with a published novel can participate.

How many novels can I submit?

One novel per contest unless the contest rules specify otherwise.

How are winners selected?

Winners are determined using reader votes, engagement and contest scoring rules.

Can I edit my novel after registering?

Yes. Minor edits are allowed while the contest is active unless restricted by the contest rules.
