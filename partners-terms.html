Author Growth Partner Programme – Summary Terms

Version MAGP-1.0 · 31 October 2026

This page is a summary for applicants. The signed Partner Agreement and any published programme policy govern participation; obtain independent legal/tax advice where appropriate.

1. Relationship

Partners participate on a flexible, independent, performance-based basis. There is no fixed salary, joining fee or guarantee of work, acceptance or earnings. The actual working relationship and applicable law determine legal classification.

2. Commission

Commission is 10% of the eligible platform share actually realised by MyLikith from an approved author referral during that author's 12-month attribution period. It is not 10% of total reader spend or author earnings. Coin purchases alone do not qualify. Refunds, chargebacks, reversals, taxes collected for government, free/promotional activity and non-attributable revenue are excluded.

3. Reconciliation and payout

Commissions are subject to monthly reconciliation and approval. The minimum payout request is ₹500. Approved statements are payable within 15 business days, subject to verification and applicable tax/withholding requirements. Lower balances carry forward.

4. Attribution

Only genuine, approved author referrals recorded by MyLikith qualify. Existing accounts, duplicate/self-referrals, fraudulent attribution and referrals not approved by MyLikith are excluded. Attribution lasts 12 months from approval/activation as recorded in the system.

5. Partner conduct

Partners must not make misleading income claims, collect joining fees, manipulate registrations or reading activity, misuse personal data, infringe copyright, or make unauthorised promises or changes to author content. Concerns must be escalated to MyLikith.

6. Intellectual property

Authors retain their manuscript rights subject to the separate publishing terms they accept with MyLikith. Partners receive no ownership or licence in author manuscripts by virtue of referring an author.

7. Leaving the programme

If a partner leaves voluntarily, commission may continue for the remainder of the original 12-month attribution period for valid referred authors, except where termination is due to fraud, serious misconduct or material breach, subject to the signed agreement and applicable law.

8. Changes and disputes

MyLikith may update programme procedures with notice. Material commission changes should not retroactively reduce already-earned commissions. The signed agreement should specify governing law, jurisdiction and notice details.
