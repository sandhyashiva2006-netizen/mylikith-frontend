Independent, part-time programme. No fixed working hours and no joining fee.

12-month attribution

Approved author referrals are attributed for 12 months, subject to programme terms.

How it works

  1. Apply using your existing MyLikith account.
  2. Our team reviews your application.
  3. After approval, receive your unique partner code.
  4. Introduce genuine authors and help them get started.
  5. Eligible commissions are reconciled monthly. Minimum payout request: ₹500.

Commission basics

Commission is 10% of the eligible platform share actually realised by MyLikith—not 10% of total reader spending or author earnings. Coin purchases alone do not qualify. Refunds, chargebacks, promotional/free activity and other excluded transactions do not qualify. Payments are subject to verification, reconciliation, applicable taxes and the programme agreement.

Who can apply?

Applicants should be at least 18, comfortable communicating with authors, and interested in literature and digital publishing. Telugu-language outreach is currently prioritised.
